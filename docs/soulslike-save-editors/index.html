Soulslike and Metroidvania save editors

Universal Save Editor opens the saves of 19 soulslikes and Metroidvanias, where runes, souls and upgrade materials are the usual edits. Pick your game to see what it can change and where its saves are. It's free, works offline and backs up every save before changing it.
