What you can edit

Squad: scrap, weapon parts and medpacks, the Ark's artifact points, population and levels, the squad level, and each squad member's level, XP, health, sanity and mutation points, with a Max crafting materials button that raises scrap and weapon parts to 999 (an estimate), leaving artifacts and gear alone (checked on a public save, not yet tried in the game)

Where Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden keeps its saves

Folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\ZoneUE4\Saved\SaveGames\Session<N>

Files: Slot<N>.sav, Autosave.sav

You don't need to find it yourself: choose Find my game's saves in the editor, pick Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden, and it lists the saves it finds. (The browser version can't look through your folders: open the file from this folder yourself.)

How to edit your Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden save

  1. Download Universal Save Editor for Windows or Linux; just run it. Rather not install anything? Use it in your browser instead: it runs on your computer too, and saving downloads the edited file.
  2. Close the game, so it doesn't write over your changes.
  3. Choose Find my game's saves, pick Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden and open your save (or drag the save file onto the window).
  4. Change what you want, then Save. The editor checks the new file and backs up the old one first.

Questions about Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden saves

Where is the Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden save file?

Usually in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ZoneUE4\Saved\SaveGames\Session<N>, in files named Slot<N>.sav, Autosave.sav. The downloaded editor finds it for you: choose Find my game's saves and pick Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden.

Will Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden still load an edited save?

The editor reads and writes this save correctly on the test saves it was checked with, but loading an edited save in the game itself hasn't been tried yet. The original is backed up before every save, so if the game doesn't accept the edit, restore it from Backups.

Can I edit Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden saves on Steam Deck or Linux?

Yes. There's a Linux build, and Find my game's saves also looks inside the Proton and Wine prefixes where Steam Play keeps Windows games' saves.

Can I edit my Mutant Year Zero: Road to Eden save in the browser?

Yes. The browser version runs on your own computer and never uploads the save. Open the file yourself and keep a copy, because saving downloads the edited file instead of backing up the old one.

Is it safe to use with online play?

Edited saves are for single-player play. Don't use them in online or multiplayer modes, where they can break rules or get an account flagged.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free and always will be: no ads, no account and no limits. The code is open source under the GPL-3.0.
